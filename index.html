The Legacy pipeline

From messy reality to usable intelligence.

Legacy begins with the raw observation. The source stays attached while the observation moves through the purification process.

01 / WEIGH

Assess the signal

Certainty, delivery, repetition, geography and session context are captured.

02 / COLLECT

Preserve the raw record

The statement and its metadata remain attached to the observation.

03 / REASON

Examine the context

Economic, cultural, contradiction and geographic-cognitive lenses are applied.

04 / PURIFY

Produce the Story

The reasoned observation becomes a clean, digestible Legacy Story.
