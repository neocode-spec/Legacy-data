The Legacy pipeline

From messy reality to usable intelligence.

Legacy begins with the field observation. The raw record remains important; purification adds structure, reasoning and context around it.

01 / WEIGH

Assess the signal

Delivery, repetition, geography and session context are captured as part of the observation.

02 / COLLECT

Preserve the raw record

The original observation stays attached to its metadata: person, location, topic and date.

03 / REASON

Examine the context

The observation is examined through economic, cultural, geographic-cognitive and contradiction lenses.

04 / PURIFY

Produce intelligence

The reasoned observation is compressed into a clean, digestible output with its calculated confidence.
