Mathematical Architecture ($F$-Formula)

Every published entry passes through a strict mathematical evaluation pipeline:

G_norm = (G - 11) / (22 - 11)
W = ((C * B) + R) * (1 + G_norm²) * (1 + |I| / 4)
M = W * ((Confirmations - Contradictions) / Lenses)
V = min(1, Independent Sources / 3)
F = M * V

This ensures biased, contradictory, or single-source noise gets filtered out before reaching final datasets.
